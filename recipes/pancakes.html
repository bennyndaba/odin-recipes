Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft, round breakfast foods made from a simple batter. They can be served with fruit or syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. In another bowl, mix the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan.
  5. Pour in some batter and cook until bubbles appear.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook the other side.
  7. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
