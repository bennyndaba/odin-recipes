Ugali

Description

Ugali is a traditional East African dish made from white cornmeal (maise) and water. It is a staple food in countries like Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

Ugali is typically served as a side dish alongside various stews, vegetables, or meats. It has a dense and dough-like consistency, and it is often eaten by tearing off small portions with the fingers and using them to scoop up other dishes.

Ingredients

  • White cornmeal (maise)
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Optional: Butter or oil for added flavor

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Gradually add the cornmeal to the boiling water while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  3. Reduce the heat and continue stirring until the mixture thickens and pulls away from the sides of the pot.
  4. Remove from heat and let it sit for a few minutes to firm up.
  5. Serve hot as a side dish with your favorite stew or vegetables.
