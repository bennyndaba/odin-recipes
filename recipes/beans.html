Beans

Description

Beans are a nutritious food enjoyed in many countries. They can be cooked with onions, tomatoes, and spices to make a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups beans
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Soak dried beans if needed.
  2. Boil the beans until tender.
  3. Chop the onion and tomatoes.
  4. Cook the onion and tomatoes in a little oil.
  5. Add the cooked beans and simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Add salt to taste and serve warm.
